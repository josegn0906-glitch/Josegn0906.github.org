My life as a College Soccer Player

Being a college soccer player requires dedication on and off the field. We student athletes must balance classes, training, games, recovery, and our personal lives while performing at a high level in class and on the field. For me soccer is more than just a sport; it teaches discipline, teamwork, communication, and perseverance.

Life on The Field and off The Field

During an average week of a college soccer player, you go to practice, train, do some weight training, do drills, and play small sided at the end. The key thing in soccer is consistency because every little bit helps. The life of a college soccer athlete is not just training and playing. Besides the sport, there are academic duties, social life, family, and personal things that need to be taken care of. Handling all these tasks teach athletes how to organize their lives better and become more independent.
